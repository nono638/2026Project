RAGBench

A configurable evaluation pipeline for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

RAGBench runs the full cartesian product of RAG configurations (chunker × embedder × strategy × language model), scores the results with LLM judges, and identifies optimal configurations for different constraints.

Experiment 0: Which LLM Judge Tracks Truth?

Frontier-class judges converge — version matters more than provider.

500 HotpotQA questions. 9 LLM judges. GPT-5.4 leads at r=0.605, with Claude Sonnet 4.6 (0.575) and GPT-5.4 Mini (0.553) close behind. Older Claude versions (Sonnet 4, Opus 4) score ~30% lower than current Sonnet 4.6 — model version drift outweighs provider differences.

Key Findings

From Experiment 0v3 — 500 HotpotQA questions scored by 9 LLM judges (3 Gemini + 4 Claude + 2 OpenAI).

GPT-5.4 is the most accurate LLM judge

Highest correlation with gold F1 (r=0.605) at n=500. Sonnet 4.6 (0.575) and GPT-5.4 Mini (0.553) follow.

Model version dominates provider differences

Claude Sonnet jumped from r=0.397 (4) → 0.575 (4.6) — a ~45% relative gain. Same provider, different generation.

GPT-5.4 Mini is the best value

r=0.553 at $0.0015/call — most accurate among cheap judges, and cross-validates with Sonnet 4.6 (r=0.720).

76% exact match on HotpotQA — answer quality is solid

Retrieval failures at 14%, generation failures at 10%. Pipeline works well out of the box.
